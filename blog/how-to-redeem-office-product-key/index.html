How to Redeem Your Office Product Key

Published 20 October 2026

Where to find your 25-character key

The Office product key is 25 characters, grouped in five blocks of five. You find it printed on the retail card, inside the box, or in the confirmation email if you bought Office online. If Office came pre-installed on a new PC, the key may be on a card in the packaging or already linked to the device.

Redeem the key at office.com/setup

Open your browser and go to office.com/setup — the same page is also available at microsoft365.com/setup, and both are official. Sign in with your Microsoft account, or create one if you do not have one yet. Your product key attaches to this account, so choose an account you will keep.

Type the 25-character code in the product key area, then select your country and the language you want. After the key is accepted, you can download and install Office straight away.

  1. 1Go to office.com/setup or microsoft365.com/setup.
  2. 2Sign in or create a Microsoft account.
  3. 3Enter your 25-character product key.
  4. 4Select your country and language.
  5. 5Download and install Office.

What happens after redeeming

Once redeemed, the key is permanently linked to your Microsoft account. You do not need the key again: to reinstall Office later, or to install it on another device, you simply sign in at office.com/setup and select Install Office. This is also why you should keep your account password safe.

Common product key mistakes

Most key errors come down to a handful of simple issues. Check these before assuming the key is invalid:

  1. 1Mixing up similar characters, such as 0 and O, or 1 and I.
  2. 2Entering the key on the wrong website — use only office.com/setup or microsoft365.com/setup.
  3. 3Trying to redeem a key that is already attached to a different account.
  4. 4Using a key for a different Office version or region than the one you are installing.
