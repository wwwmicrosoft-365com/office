How to Activate Microsoft Office After Installation

Published 13 October 2026

How activation works

Office activation links your installation to your Microsoft account or product key. In most cases you do nothing special: the first time you open an app like Word and sign in, the activation wizard runs by itself.

The account you sign in with must be the same one you used at office.com/setup or microsoft365.com/setup when you redeemed your key or bought your subscription.

Activate by signing in

Open any Office app, such as Word. If the activation wizard appears, choose Sign in and enter the email and password for your Microsoft account. Follow the prompts on screen — the setup goes through activation automatically and you only sign in once.

  1. 1Open Word, Excel, or another Office app.
  2. 2Select Sign in when the activation wizard appears.
  3. 3Enter the Microsoft account tied to your purchase.
  4. 4Follow the on-screen prompts to finish.

Activate with a product key

If you have a 25-character product key that is not yet redeemed, go to office.com/setup (also available at microsoft365.com/setup), sign in, and enter the key there first. Once the key is attached to your account, activating the apps is just a matter of signing in. See our product key redemption guide for the full process.

Check your activation status

To confirm Office is activated, open Word and go to File, then Account. Under Product Information you should see that the product is activated, along with the account it belongs to.

If activation fails

The most common cause is signing in with a different account than the one that holds the subscription or key. Sign out inside the app (File, then Account, then Sign out), restart the app, and sign in with the correct account. If the problem continues, our troubleshooting guide lists more fixes, and the official Microsoft Support site can check your subscription status.
