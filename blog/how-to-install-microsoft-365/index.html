How to Install Microsoft 365 on Windows and Mac

Published 6 October 2026

Before you start

Make sure you have three things ready: a Microsoft account, a valid subscription or product key, and a stable internet connection. The whole setup starts at office.com/setup — Microsoft also offers the same page at microsoft365.com/setup, and both addresses are official.

If you bought a retail copy, redeem your 25-character product key first so it attaches to your Microsoft account. Our guide on redeeming an Office product key explains that step in detail.

Install on Windows

Open your browser and go to office.com/setup or microsoft365.com/setup. Sign in with the Microsoft account tied to your purchase, then select Install Office. The installer downloads to your PC — run it and follow the on-screen directions.

When the progress bar finishes, open any app such as Word and sign in once. The setup completes activation automatically.

  1. 1Go to office.com/setup (also microsoft365.com/setup).
  2. 2Sign in with your Microsoft account.
  3. 3Select Install Office and run the downloaded installer.
  4. 4Open Word and sign in to finish activation.

Install on a Mac

On a Mac, sign in at office.com/setup and select Install Office. Open your Downloads folder and double-click the installer package (Office installer.dmg). When the first Office screen appears, select Continue, choose how you wish to install, and follow the prompts.

After the installation, open Word from Launchpad and sign in with your subscription account to activate.

Install on your phone or tablet

Activate your subscription at office.com/setup or microsoft365.com/setup first. Then open the Google Play store on Android or the App Store on iPhone and iPad, search for Microsoft 365 or Word, and tap Install. Open the app and log in with your email and password.

Microsoft 365 subscribers can install on up to five devices per person, so one subscription covers your computer, phone, and tablet.

What to do after installing

Open each app you plan to use once, so it finishes its first-run setup. If anything goes wrong during installation, our troubleshooting article covers the most common errors and their fixes. You can also follow the full step-by-step setup guide with a picture for every step.
