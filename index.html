What is office.com/setup

office.com/setup is the page where Microsoft Office users easily install, set up, and activate Office on a Windows or Mac system. Enter your product key, download the setup, and follow the steps below — with a picture for every step.

Setup at office.com/setup

The whole process in seven steps. Each step is explained with pictures below.

  1. Go to office.com/setup for Office setup.
  2. Sign in or create a new Microsoft account.
  3. Find your Office product key.
  4. Enter the Microsoft Office product key.
  5. Select your country and language.
  6. Download Office setup and follow the on-screen instructions.
  7. Finish the installation and run the applications.

How to install Office on Windows

Follow these six steps at office.com/setup. Every step links to the official Microsoft page where the action happens.

1

Go to office.com/setup

Open your browser, visit office.com/setup and select the Office product you want to install.

2

Sign in to your Microsoft account

Sign in with the email and password for the account tied to your Office purchase. If you do not have a Microsoft account yet, create one — your product key attaches to it.

3

Find your Office product key

The key is 25 characters, grouped in five blocks. You find it printed on the retail card, inside the box, or in the confirmation email if you bought Office online.

4

Enter your Microsoft Office product key

Once signed in, type the 25-character code in the product key area, then select your country and the language you want.

5

Download and install Office

Select Install Office and let the installer download. Run it and follow the on-screen directions to install your registered copy of Microsoft Office.

6

Run the activation wizard

When the install finishes, open any app such as Word. Follow the prompts on screen and sign in once — the setup goes through activation automatically.

Install Office on a Mac

  1. Go to office.com/setup, sign in with your subscription, and select Install Office.
  2. Open Downloads and double-click the installer package (Office installer.dmg).
  3. When the first Office screen appears, select Continue and sign in with your Office subscription.
  4. Choose how you wish to install the setup, then Continue and follow the prompts.
  5. Open Word from Launchpad and sign in to activate.

Uninstall Office

Windows

  1. Open the Control Panel and go to Programs.
  2. Select Uninstall a program and find Office in the list.
  3. Choose Uninstall and wait for it to finish.
  4. Restart the computer when asked.

Mac

  1. Open your Applications folder and find the Office folder.
  2. Double-click the Office uninstaller tool.
  3. Tick the Office apps to remove and select Uninstall.

Office setup on Android, iPhone & iPad

Android

  • Activate your subscription at office.com/setup first.
  • Open the Google Play store.
  • Search for Microsoft 365 or Word.
  • Tap Install, then Open.

iPhone & iPad

  • Activate your subscription at office.com/setup first.
  • Open the App Store.
  • Search for Microsoft Word and tap Get.
  • Open the app and log in with your email and password.

Sign in with the account on your subscription to unlock full editing. Microsoft 365 subscribers can install on up to five devices per person.

Quick answers

Where do I enter my Office product key?
At office.com/setup, while signed in to the Microsoft account you want the licence on. Enter the full 25-character code, hyphens included. Remember the account — you reinstall from it later without the key.
How do I reinstall Microsoft Office?
Sign in at account.microsoft.com/services, open Services & subscriptions, and select Install Office. No key needed — the licence lives on your account.
Microsoft 365 or Office 2021?
Microsoft 365 is a subscription that always has the latest apps and can be shared with up to six people. Office 2021 is a one-time purchase for one PC or Mac and cannot be upgraded — you buy the next release at full price.
Renew or turn off recurring billing
Renew at office.com/renew. To stop auto-renewal, open Services & subscriptions, choose Manage, then Turn off recurring billing.
